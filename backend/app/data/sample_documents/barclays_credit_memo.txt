BARCLAYS CORPORATE & INVESTMENT BANKING
CREDIT UNDERWRITING MEMORANDUM - ANNUAL FACILITY REVIEW
Borrower: Apex Industrial Holdings Ltd.
Facility: $450,000,000 Senior Secured Revolving Credit Facility & Term Loan B
Date: September 12, 2024
Risk Rating: Satisfactory (Risk Grade 4)

1. FACILITY OVERVIEW & PURPOSE
Apex Industrial Holdings operates specialized automation component manufacturing for tier-1 automotive and aerospace OEMs. The facility provides $250M Term Loan B and $200M revolving working capital facility to support manufacturing facility expansion in Eastern Europe.

2. FINANCIAL CONDITION & COVENANT PERFORMANCE
- Consolidated Revenue: Reported revenue of $840.5 million for the trailing twelve months, up 8.2% year-over-year.
- Adjusted EBITDA: Generated EBITDA of $112.4 million, maintaining an EBITDA margin of 13.4%.
- Total Debt Outstanding: Senior funded debt stands at $315.0 million. Net debt is $265.0 million after deducting $50.0 million in unrestricted cash.
- Leverage Ratio: Senior Net Leverage Ratio measured 2.36x, well within the maximum covenant threshold of 3.75x.
- Fixed Charge & Interest Coverage: Minimum Interest Coverage ratio stood at 4.82x versus required covenant test of 3.00x.
- Free Cash Flow: Operating cash flow generated $74.2 million; capital expenditures totaled $28.5 million, yielding free cash flow of $45.7 million.

3. COVENANT COMPLIANCE & UNDERWRITER ASSESSMENT
All financial covenants (Debt-to-EBITDA, Interest Coverage, Liquidity Minimum of $25M) are in full compliance with no waiver requests. Liquidity reserves stand at over $30 billion across affiliated holding structures with undrawn credit facilities of $135 million.
Underwriting Recommendation: RE-APPROVE facility at current pricing margin (SOFR + 225 bps) with scheduled semi-annual monitoring.
