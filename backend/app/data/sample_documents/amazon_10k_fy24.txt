PART II - ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Executive Overview
Net sales increased 11% to $620.13 billion in fiscal 2024, compared with $574.79 billion in fiscal 2023. Excluding the $2.3 billion unfavorable impact from year-over-year foreign exchange rates, net sales increased 12%.

Segment Financial Performance
- AWS Segment net sales increased 19% year-over-year to $105.25 billion, compared with $88.52 billion in fiscal 2023. AWS segment operating income was $39.82 billion, representing an AWS operating margin of 37.8%, driven by structural optimization of server life cycles and accelerated customer commitments for enterprise cloud migrations.
- North America segment net sales increased 9% to $378.50 billion, with operating income of $21.40 billion.
- International segment net sales increased 8% to $136.38 billion, with operating income turning positive to $7.20 billion.

Consolidated Earnings & Cash Flows
Consolidated operating income improved substantially to $68.42 billion, compared with $36.85 billion in fiscal 2023. Consolidated operating margin reached 11.0%.
Consolidated net income was $49.21 billion, or $4.68 per diluted share.
Adjusted EBITDA totaled $104.50 billion.
Operating cash flow generated $112.70 billion. Capital expenditures totaled $65.00 billion, resulting in free cash flow of $47.70 billion for the trailing twelve months.
Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities were $86.78 billion. Total long-term debt was $58.30 billion.

ITEM 1A. INSTITUTIONAL RISK FACTORS
- Infrastructure capital commitments: Substantial investments in custom silicon and AI infrastructure may lengthen payback horizons if enterprise workload adoption slows.
- Regulatory antitrust and competition laws: Global scrutiny under the EU Digital Markets Act and Federal Trade Commission proceedings in the United States.
- International trade restrictions and shipping volatility: Exposure to ocean freight rate fluctuations and potential customs tariffs impacting retail import unit economics.
